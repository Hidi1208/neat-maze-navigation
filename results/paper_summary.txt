NEAT DRONE GENERALISATION — FULL PAPER SUMMARY
=======================================================

ABSTRACT RECAP
----------------------------------------
NEAT is independently applied to 100 simulated maze puzzles.
The best-performing genome from each run is extracted.
These 100 genomes are averaged to form a single generalised controller.
The controller is then tested on an unseen 101st maze.

ENVIRONMENT
----------------------------------------
Simulator    : Custom Python/Pygame drone simulator
Maze size    : 1000 x 600 pixels
Obstacles    : Procedurally generated vertical walls with gaps
Sensors      : 7-ray LIDAR (ray scattering) at angles [-70, -40, -15, 0, 15, 40, 70] degrees
Control      : Hybrid (goal-directed autopilot + NEAT correction)
Inputs       : 7 LIDAR readings + goal dx + goal dy + angle + velocity = 11
Outputs      : 1 (steering correction)

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
The aggregated genome did not reach the goal in the unseen maze.
However, it survived 130 frames and reached
within 618.31 px of the goal.
This indicates partial generalisation. Further training iterations
or a lower majority threshold may improve cross-environment transfer.
